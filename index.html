About this directory

Cebu Churches is being built as a free public reference for parishioners, visitors, and pilgrims — a single place to look up a church's location, its assigned priest, its official Facebook page, and its Mass schedule, instead of hunting across dozens of scattered posts and outdated blogs.

This is Phase 1: the parishes, shrines, and chapels of Cebu City, Mandaue City, and Lapu-Lapu City. Later phases will extend coverage to the rest of Cebu Province — Talisay, Naga, Carcar, the northern towns, Bantayan, and Camotes.

Help us keep it accurate

Parish details — especially the assigned priest, Facebook page, and Mass times — change more often than any directory can track alone. Fields marked "being verified" are ones we haven't independently confirmed yet.

If you're a parish secretary, priest, or parishioner and can help confirm or correct a listing, we'd welcome it — this directory is meant to be maintained with the community it serves, not built once and left to go stale.
